Peroxisome Structure

Peroxisomes are single-membrane-bound organelles found in eukaryotic cells. They contain enzymes for metabolic processes like β-oxidation of fatty acids and detoxification of hydrogen peroxide.

Substrates
Enzymes

Peroxisome Functions

Peroxisomes break down fatty acids through β-oxidation, detoxify harmful hydrogen peroxide using catalase, and are involved in the synthesis of bile acids and plasmalogens.

Peroxisome Disorders

Zellweger syndrome is a genetic disorder caused by defects in peroxisome biogenesis. Patients lack functional peroxisomes, leading to accumulation of very long-chain fatty acids and severe neurological symptoms.

How to Play: Assembly Section

In the Assembly section, your goal is to build a complete peroxisome by dragging and dropping the correct components into their designated slots.

  • Drag & Drop: Select a component from the "Toolbox" at the bottom of the screen. Drag it over to the peroxisome in the "Workspace" and release it over an empty slot.
  • Component Information: Hover over (or tap and hold on mobile) a component in the toolbox to see its name and a brief description.
  • Validate: Once you think you have placed all the necessary components, click the "Validate" button to check your assembly.
  • Reset: If you want to start over, click the "Reset" button to clear all placed components.
  • Score: You earn points for correctly placing components.
  • Progress: The progress bar at the bottom indicates how many components you have successfully placed.
